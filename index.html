Private Sri Lanka TukTuk Tours in Negombo & Colombo

Welcome to Ceylon Tuk Tuk Tours. We provide authentic, private TukTuk tours in Negombo, Colombo city sightseeing excursions, Muthurajawela lagoon mangrove safaris, and custom Sri Lanka day tours with Anthony Appuhamy.

Featured Sri Lanka Tour Packages

Negombo Coastal & Village TukTuk Tour (3 Hours)

Experience Negombo's coastal lifestyle in a classic Sri Lankan three-wheeler. Highlights include Lellama fish market, outrigger catamarans, beach fish drying coir mats, quiet coconut palm village lanes, and fresh King Coconut by the ocean.

Negombo Historic City & Cultural Heritage Tour (3.5 Hours)

Explore Negombo's Little Rome history: 17th-century Dutch Fort stone portal, Hamilton Canal lock system, vaulted ceiling frescoes at St. Mary's Catholic Church, Negombo produce market, and the 6-meter dragon entrance of Angurukaramulla Buddhist Temple.

Colombo City Highlights & Street Food TukTuk Tour (4.5 Hours)

Discover Sri Lanka's energetic capital. Ride through Pettah bazaar spice streets, the candy-striped Red Mosque (Jami Ul-Alfar), Gangaramaya Buddhist Temple on Beira Lake, Independence Memorial Hall, and authentic street food snacks at Galle Face Green.

Muthurajawela Lagoon & Mangrove Boat Safari (4 Hours)

A tranquil nature trip bordering Negombo. Includes a scenic TukTuk ride to the Muthurajawela marshlands, a private covered motorboat safari through mangrove canals, birdwatching (kingfishers, herons, cormorants), and observing Asian water monitors.

Explore Sri Lanka Your Way – Multi-Day Private Tours & South Coast

Customizable private Sri Lanka itineraries: 7-Day Sri Lanka Explorer, 12-Day Sri Lanka Discovery, 20-Day Sri Lanka Grand Journey, and South Coast experiences (Weligama surfing, Hiriketiya, Mirissa whale watching, Galle Fort, Unawatuna). Travel comfortably by air-conditioned car or van for longer journeys, with classic Tuk Tuk rides for local sightseeing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can you pick us up for the tours?
We offer complimentary door-to-door pickup from any hotel or villa in Negombo, Waikkal, Katunayake, or Bandaranaike International Airport arrivals.
What happens if it rains during a TukTuk tour?
Our TukTuks are fully equipped with transparent, roll-down waterproof storm curtains that keep guests dry while allowing clear sightseeing.
How do tour pricing and payment work?
We believe in transparent local pricing with zero middleman commissions. Payment is made in cash directly at the end of the tour in LKR, USD, EUR, or GBP.
